Smartsheet Controller

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

Magoo Media (“Magoo Media,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) provides Smartsheet Controller, an independent Stream Deck plugin for managing Smartsheet work. This Privacy Policy explains what information the plugin may process and how it is handled.

Information the plugin processes

Depending on the actions you use, Smartsheet Controller may process Smartsheet account authorization information and Smartsheet data needed to complete your request, such as sheet, row, column, contact, status, comment, update-request, due-date, summary, and template information.

Authorization and permissions

You authorize the plugin to access Smartsheet through Smartsheet’s authorization flow. The plugin is intended to operate only within the permissions granted by your Smartsheet account. Magoo Media does not ask for or store your Smartsheet password.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate your connection, display the Smartsheet information you request, and perform the Smartsheet actions you initiate from Stream Deck. We do not use Smartsheet content for advertising or sell it to third parties.

Sharing and service providers

Requests are sent to Smartsheet and its services as needed to perform your selected actions. If you contact support, the information you submit through the support form is handled by the form provider and used to respond to your request.

Storage, retention, and deletion

The plugin does not intentionally maintain a separate product database of your Smartsheet content. Local connection settings and authorization data may remain until you disconnect the account, remove the plugin, or clear the plugin’s settings. You can also revoke the connection through Smartsheet. Any temporary authorization data used by a future public-release service will be limited to maintaining the connection and will be handled according to that release’s approved architecture.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the plugin and its operating environment. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the plugin’s public release configuration changes. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, use the Magoo Media support form.

Smartsheet Controller is independent software and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Smartsheet Inc.
